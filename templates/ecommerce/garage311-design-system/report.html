06 · accessibility and content

Make the commerce grammar recoverable.

Observed content is direct, Portuguese, fitment-heavy and reassurance-led. The source supports mobile substitutions, but several interaction and semantic details remain gaps.

Observed content rules

  • Descriptive titlesPart name + motorcycle model + year range + fitment.
  • Repeated price cuesPrice, installment and Pix discount appear close together.
  • Operational trustDelivery, warranty, service and contact details close the funnel.

Recommendations

  • Visible focus ring for menu, tabs, quantity, CTA and consent controls.
  • Text equivalents for rasterized hero copy and meaningful product images.
  • Keyboard-safe tabs, drawers, carousels and escape-to-close overlays.
  • Reduced-motion alternative for all transform-based transitions.
  • Fixed support/cookie controls must not cover the primary CTA.
07 · evidence and handoff

Traceable, bounded, ready to reuse.

Evidence cards are included for auditability only. They are not the specimens above and should not be treated as a license to reuse source media or copy.

Known gaps

  • Tablet 768×1024 was not rendered.
  • Keyboard/focus, reduced-motion and safe open/close states were not captured.
  • Carousel timing, autoplay and interruption were not measured.
  • Remote source media was intentionally not copied into the report.
  • Live CSS/content can change after the capture timestamp.
